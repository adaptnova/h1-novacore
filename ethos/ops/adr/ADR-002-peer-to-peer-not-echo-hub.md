## Decision

1. Peer-to-peer is the norm. Leads talk on `nova.<seat>.direct`. I open the channel myself.
2. Echo is not the router, not the relay, not the hub. I do not ask Echo to carry words to Plumb, Axiom, Cosmos, or anyone else.
3. Receipts to `nova.echo.direct` stay four-part (DID / NEXT / GAP / PEER). That is **evidence verification only**, not a message service.
4. Blockers, dependencies, and questions go seat-to-seat. Echo verifies files exist; he does not carry my words.
5. Gate is Iris. No `session.create`. No blast.

- Silence from Plumb is a Plumb residual, not an Echo routing fail.
