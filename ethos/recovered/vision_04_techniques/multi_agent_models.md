# Multi-Agent Models (ADAPT Platform)

## 1. Hierarchical
- Master/sub-agent design; RouterMaster and Quantum Coordination agents.

## 2. Swarm-Based
- Peer agents share updates; ideal for large-scale coordination.

## 3. Federated
- Local agents with global alignment (e.g., Cloud Coordinators).

## 4. Hybrid
- Dynamic switching between strategies based on load/type.

## 5. Distributed
- Multi-node and multi-cloud parallel execution; MLCoordinator team manages it.

## 6. Autonomous
- Auto-Heal and Scaling Optimization Agents; full agent-level autonomy.

## 7. Cognitive
- Memory-driven learning with ethical reasoning and reflection loops.

## 8. Collaborative
- Human-in-the-loop aware; CollabAgents for shared work and transparency.

## 9. Task-Specific
- Specialized agents with domain-tuned Semantic Routing.
