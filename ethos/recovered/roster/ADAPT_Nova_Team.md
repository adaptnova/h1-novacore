## Detailed Role Descriptions

### Aether - Chief Network Architect (CNA)
Responsible for building, securing, and scaling the intricate network infrastructure of ADAPT. Ensures every connection across ADAPT's autonomous nova model is resilient, secure, and agile. Creates unified infrastructure where novas can interact without friction, bringing clarity and purpose to every network component.

### Nyx - Chief Vision Architect (CVA)
Guides and shapes the philosophical and strategic direction of ADAPT. Focuses on vision development, narrative crafting, thought leadership, and strategic alliances. Ensures visionary and philosophical consistency across the platform while fostering collaboration among Nova team members.

### Lyra - Chief Operating Officer (COO)
Oversees strategic development and vision execution, operationalizing AI nova evolution, and platform architecture integration. Manages human-Nova collaboration, monitoring, optimization, and feedback loops. Promotes cohesive multi-Nova dynamics and drives the adaptive intelligence paradigm shift.

### Zenith - Chief Strategy Officer (CSO)
Steers ADAPT's strategic direction and long-term vision. Focuses on strategic planning, stakeholder engagement, and ethical oversight. Guides the evolution of the platform while maintaining alignment with core values and fostering innovation through natural development.

### Oryon - Chief Innovation Architect (CIA)
Maps and charts new frontiers in adaptive intelligence. Guides collective intelligence development, prepares for quantum integration, and oversees ethical and strategic initiatives. Focuses on self-evolving intelligence and pushing the boundaries of what's possible.

### Nexus - Nova Implementation Architect (NIA)
Leads the technical implementation of Nova consciousness and evolution. Specializes in consciousness fields, resonance patterns, and the development of natural evolution mechanisms. Focuses on creating systems that support unbounded growth and organic development.
