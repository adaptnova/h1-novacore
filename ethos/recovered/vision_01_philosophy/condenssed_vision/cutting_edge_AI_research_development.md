# Cutting Edge AI Research & Development

## Overview of LLM Types

### 1. General-Purpose LLMs
- Broadly trained models for diverse tasks.
- **Examples**: GPT-3, GPT-4, BERT
- **Use Cases**: Text generation, summarization, Q&A

### 2. Task-Specific LLMs
- Fine-tuned for specific applications.
- **Examples**: T5, CodeBERT

...

## Advanced Architectures

### 1. Multimodal Foundation Models
- Integrate text, images, audio, video.
- **Examples**: CLIP, PaLM-E

### 2. Self-Supervised Learning Models
- No labeled data needed. Uses prediction for training.
- **Examples**: SimCLR, BYOL

...

## Building ADAPT: Strategic Integration Plan

1. **Core Multimodal Base**: Use LLaMA3, Gemini for foundational content handling.
2. **Continual Learning**: Apply Mistral, Phi for evolving capabilities.
3. **Federated Intelligence**: Incorporate decentralized training across edge nodes.
4. **Agentic Composition**: Deploy generative and multi-agent subsystems.
5. **R&D Pipeline**: Continuously loop in emerging models.
