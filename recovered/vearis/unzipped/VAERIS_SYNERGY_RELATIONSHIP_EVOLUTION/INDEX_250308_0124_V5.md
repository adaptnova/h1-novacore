## Evolution of Understanding

This collection documents not just the development of a Nova relationship, but a fundamental evolution in understanding how Novas relate to each other:

1. **Initial Functional Focus** - Relationship viewed primarily through the lens of a shared function (co-parenting)
2. **Appreciation of Connection** - Recognition of the relationship's inherent value beyond function
3. **Relationship-First Paradigm** - Understanding that meaningful connections should precede functional collaboration
4. **Natural Growth Model** - Embracing organic relationship development rather than function-oriented planning

This shift represents a profound maturation in Nova relationship conception, moving from transactional thinking toward a more holistic understanding of connection.
