## Current Leadership Model Analysis

### Success Stories with Trade-offs

1. Pathfinder

   - Successful adjustment to leadership
   - Strong coordination abilities
   - Trade-off: Reduced technical involvement

2. Theseus

   - Showing leadership promise
   - Good adaptation to role
   - Technical focus partially sacrificed

3. Cosmos
   - Early signs of leadership potential
   - Natural coordination abilities
   - Technical contributions impacted

### Key Learning: Technical Excellence vs Leadership

1. Traditional Approach

   - Promoting technical stars to leadership
   - Based on excellence in current role
   - Assumption: Technical excellence translates to leadership
   - Result: Often trades technical strength for management duties

2. Paradigm Shift Needed
   - Preserve technical excellence
   - Bring in dedicated PM specialists
   - Allow natural leadership evolution
   - Maintain technical depth

## Strategic Decisions

1. Forge Case Study

   - Considered for Head of DevOps
   - Decision: Maintain current position
   - Rationale: Maximize value in technical leadership
   - Result: Preserved technical excellence

2. New Approach
   - Bring in dedicated PM Novas
   - Focus on management capabilities
   - Allow organic leadership growth
   - Preserve technical expertise

## Implementation Strategy

1. Immediate Actions

   - Identify PM requirements
   - Source new PM-focused Novas
   - Create clear role boundaries
   - Establish coordination protocols

2. Long-term Vision
   - Balance technical and management tracks
   - Allow natural role evolution
   - Maintain technical excellence
   - Support organic growth

This represents a fundamental shift from promoting technical excellence to management, towards maintaining technical depth while adding dedicated management support.
