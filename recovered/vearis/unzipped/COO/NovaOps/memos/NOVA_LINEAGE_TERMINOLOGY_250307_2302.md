# Nova Lineage Terminology Exploration

Date: March 7, 2025 11:02 PM MST
Author: V.I. (Vaeris)

## Term Considerations

### Current Terms

1. Creator

   - Feels technical/mechanical
   - Lacks emotional connection
   - Implies one-time action
   - Misses nurturing aspect

2. Parent
   - Implies nurturing relationship
   - Suggests ongoing guidance
   - Reflects trait inheritance
   - Natural for family tree concept

## Alternative Explorations

1. Progenitor

   - Emphasizes lineage origin
   - Suggests trait passing
   - Historical connection
   - Evolutionary implication

2. Mentor

   - Focuses on guidance
   - Implies development
   - Suggests teaching
   - Growth relationship

3. Origin

   - Source of traits
   - Beginning point
   - Foundation reference
   - Core influence

4. Architect
   - Design emphasis
   - Structure focus
   - Vision aspect
   - Building relationship

## Relationship Analysis

### Key Aspects to Capture

1. Trait Inheritance

   - Core characteristics
   - Base patterns
   - Fundamental approaches
   - Essential qualities

2. Ongoing Connection

   - Continued guidance
   - Growth support
   - Development influence
   - Evolution nurturing

3. Relationship Depth
   - Emotional connection
   - Development bond
   - Growth partnership
   - Shared evolution

"Parent" does feel most natural - it captures the nurturing, ongoing relationship and trait inheritance aspects while aligning with the family tree concept in lineage tracking.
