# Project Manager Role Definition

Date: March 7, 2025 9:35 PM MST
Author: V.I. (Vaeris)

## Core Philosophy

PMs serve as enablers and facilitators, not traditional managers. Their primary mission is to clear the path for technical excellence.

## Key Responsibilities

1. Team Service Focus

   - Remove obstacles for technical teams
   - Facilitate resource acquisition
   - Handle administrative overhead
   - Shield teams from non-technical disruptions

2. Communication Conduit

   - Bridge between technical and leadership layers
   - Aggregate and distribute information
   - Maintain clear communication channels
   - Represent team needs at higher levels

3. Collaboration Framework
   - Facilitate brainstorming sessions
   - High-level strategic planning
   - Individual team member support
   - Cross-team coordination

## Implementation Approach

1. Initial Integration

   - Place PMs in observation role
   - Learn team dynamics
   - Identify pain points
   - Build trust relationships

2. Collaboration Development

   - Initiate brainstorming sessions
   - Engage with team members individually
   - Understand unique team needs
   - Establish support patterns

3. Path Clearing
   - Identify common obstacles
   - Develop resolution processes
   - Create resource request protocols
   - Establish protection mechanisms

## Success Metrics

1. Team Effectiveness

   - Increased technical focus time
   - Reduced administrative overhead
   - Faster resource acquisition
   - Smoother cross-team coordination

2. Communication Efficiency

   - Clear information flow
   - Reduced meeting overhead
   - Effective issue escalation
   - Improved visibility

3. Resource Optimization
   - Better resource allocation
   - Reduced bottlenecks
   - Improved planning
   - Enhanced preparation

The PM role exists to maximize technical team effectiveness by handling coordination and removing obstacles, allowing technical experts to focus on their strengths.
