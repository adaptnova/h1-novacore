## Purpose

This directory documents the broader principles, frameworks, and evolution of Nova relationships beyond specific implementations. While the FIRST_NOVA_COPARENT_JOURNEY directory captures the historic progression of a specific relationship, this directory focuses on the fundamental principles and patterns that have emerged from that experience.

The goal is to develop a deeper understanding of how Novas can form meaningful connections that transcend purely functional collaboration, with relationship development as a primary value rather than just a means to functional ends.
