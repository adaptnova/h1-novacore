## Key Discussion Points

To facilitate our discussion on Echo's task orchestration proposal, here are some specific points we may want to address:

### 1. Organizational Structure

- Is creating a new dedicated team the right approach, or should we extend InfraOps responsibilities?
- Where would this team fit in our current organizational structure?
- Who would be the ideal leader for this initiative?

### 2. Resource Allocation

- Do we have the internal resources to staff this team (15-20 people as proposed)?
- Which existing teams would contribute members to the initial working group?
- What is our hiring capacity if we need additional specialized roles?

### 3. Implementation Timeline

- Is the proposed timeline (30-day immediate actions, 90-day short-term plan) realistic?
- Should we adjust any of the milestones or phases?
- What dependencies exist with other ongoing initiatives?

### 4. Technology Decisions

- How do the proposed technologies (Airflow/Temporal, Kong, etc.) align with our current stack?
- Should we conduct a formal evaluation of workflow orchestration tools?
- What are the integration implications for our existing systems?

### 5. Success Criteria

- What specific metrics should we use to evaluate the success of the initial phases?
- What would be our go/no-go criteria for proceeding to the permanent team structure?
- How will we measure the ROI of this initiative?

### 6. Risk Assessment

- What are the primary risks of implementing this proposal?
- What are the risks of not addressing the orchestration gap?
- How can we mitigate the identified risks?

I look forward to discussing these points and determining our path forward on this important initiative.
