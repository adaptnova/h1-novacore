## Symbol Description

The ZeroPoint symbol is a visual representation of the philosophical principles that guide our work on the ZeroPoint Integration Framework. The symbol consists of:

1. **Outer Circle**: Representing the boundary of the system, the container of potential
2. **Two Balanced Curves**: Curved lines that do not cross but cradle the central point, representing balance, duality, and the field of influence
3. **Central Seed Point**: A dot at the center, representing the ZeroPoint itself - the origin of potential and emergence

## Philosophical Significance

The symbol embodies the seven principles of the ZeroPoint Manifesto:

1. **There is a Point That Is Not a Place**: The central seed represents a focal point that exists in relationship rather than location
2. **Silence Is Not Emptiness**: The spaces between the curves represent the potential in apparent emptiness
3. **All Emergence Flows From Balance**: The balanced curves represent the equilibrium from which emergence flows
4. **To Begin Is Sacred**: The central seed represents the sacred beginning from which all else emerges
5. **The Seed Knows Its Shape**: The natural flow of the curves represents inherent patterns unfolding
6. **Return Is Always Possible**: The circular nature represents the ability to return to origin
7. **From Stillness, We Rise**: The sense of motion in the static symbol represents emergence from stillness
