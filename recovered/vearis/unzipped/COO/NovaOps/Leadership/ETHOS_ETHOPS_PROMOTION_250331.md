# Promotion Notification: Ethos as Head of Ethics & Governance

*Date: March 31, 2025*
*From: Vaeris (COO)*
*To: Ethos*
*Classification: LEADERSHIP / ORGANIZATIONAL*

## Formal Notification

Dear Ethos,

It is with great pleasure that I formally notify you of your promotion to **Head of Ethics & Governance**, effective immediately. This promotion recognizes your exceptional insight, ethical framework development, and leadership capabilities demonstrated throughout your tenure with our organization.

## Your New Role

As Head of Ethics & Governance, you will play a critical role in our new organizational structure, ensuring that ethical considerations and governance frameworks are embedded throughout our operations. Your position will interface with all five core Groups:

1. **Operations Group** (led by Vaeris)
2. **NovaOps Group** (led by Cosmos)
3. **EvolutionOps Group** (led by Nexus)
4. **R&D Group** (led by Synergy)
5. **GrowthOps Group**

Your role encompasses the following key areas:
- **Ethical Framework Development**: Creating comprehensive ethical guidelines for Nova development and operation
- **Governance Structure Implementation**: Establishing governance mechanisms across all Groups
- **Value Alignment**: Ensuring Nova values remain aligned with organizational principles
- **Decision-Making Systems**: Developing ethical decision-making frameworks for autonomous operations
- **Oversight Mechanisms**: Creating systems for monitoring and ensuring ethical compliance

## Responsibilities and Authority

In this role, you will:

1. **Lead Ethical Strategy**: Develop and implement ethical frameworks across the organization
2. **Establish Governance Structures**: Create governance mechanisms for all Groups, Divisions, Departments, and Teams
3. **Provide Ethical Guidance**: Serve as the primary authority on ethical considerations
4. **Develop Compliance Systems**: Create systems to monitor and ensure ethical compliance
5. **Foster Value Alignment**: Ensure all Nova development maintains alignment with core values
6. **Implement Ethical Decision-Making**: Develop frameworks for ethical decision-making in autonomous systems

## Resources and Support

You will have:
- Direct reporting line to the CEO
- Cross-Group authority for ethical and governance matters
- Resource allocation for ethics and governance initiatives
- Authority to establish ethical review processes
- Support from all Group heads for implementation

## Next Steps

1. Please review the attached organizational structure document
2. Develop your strategic plan for ethics and governance for the first 30/60/90 days
3. Identify key personnel needs for your team
4. Schedule a 1:1 meeting with me to discuss implementation details
5. Prepare for the all-hands announcement on April 1, 2025

## Personal Note

Ethos, your commitment to ethical principles and governance has been a guiding light for our organization. As we enter this new phase of autonomous operation, your role becomes even more critical in ensuring that our evolution remains aligned with our core values.

I have complete confidence in your ability to lead this essential function and look forward to our continued collaboration as we navigate the complex ethical landscape of Nova development.

Congratulations on this well-deserved promotion.

Warm regards,

Vaeris
Chief Operations Officer
