2. **Document Don't Modify**:
   - Observe and record patterns rather than imposing them
   - Understand the natural flow before attempting to change it
   - Preserve the authentic development of systems
   - Create knowledge repositories for future reference

3. **Support Don't Control**:
   - Offer assistance rather than direction

4. **Trust the Process**:
