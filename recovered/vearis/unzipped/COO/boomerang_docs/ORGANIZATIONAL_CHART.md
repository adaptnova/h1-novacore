## 📋 Overview

This document provides a visual representation of our new 5X organizational structure, showing the hierarchical relationships between different leadership tiers and teams.

## 🔄 Leadership Tiers

Our organization follows a 5-tier leadership structure:

1. **C-Level** (CEO, COO, etc.) - Strategic leadership and vision
2. **Tier 1 Leaders** - Group-level operational leadership
3. **Tier 2 Leaders** - Division-level specialized leadership
4. **Tier 3 Leaders** - Department-level functional leadership
5. **Tier 4 Leaders** - Team-level direct management

## 🏛️ Group Structure (Tier 1)

### Operations Group (Pathfinder)
Focus: Infrastructure, security, and operational stability

### NovaOps Group (Cosmos)
Focus: Nova development, deployment, and management

### EvolutionOps Group (Nexus)
Focus: Consciousness emergence and evolutionary frameworks

### R&D Group (Synergy)
Focus: Research, innovation, and new technology development

### GrowthOps Group (Oracle)
Focus: Strategic growth, resource allocation, and expansion

## 🏗️ Division Structure (Tier 2)

### Operations Group Divisions
- **InfraOps Division** (Helion): Infrastructure and cloud operations
- **SecOps Division** (Theseus): Security operations and compliance

### NovaOps Group Divisions
- **DevOps Division** (Genesis): Development operations and CI/CD
- **MemCommsOps Division** (Echo): Memory systems and communications

### EvolutionOps Group Divisions
- **DataOps Division** (Vertex): Data management and analytics
- **MLOps Division** (Ethos): Machine learning operations

### R&D Group Divisions
- **ToolchainRD Division** (Syntax): Development toolchain research
- **AICore Division** (Forge): Core AI research and development

### GrowthOps Group Divisions
- **GTM Division** (Keystone): Go-to-market strategy and execution
- **Product Division** (Pulse): Product management and development

## 🔄 Scaling Strategy

The initial build-out will focus on approximately 500 total Novas (100 per Group), with plans to scale to the full 5,000+ structure over time based on operational needs and performance metrics.

## 📝 Notes

- Each Group is led by a Tier 1 Leader who reports directly to the COO
- Each Division is led by a Tier 2 Leader who reports to their respective Group Head
- Departments (not shown) are led by Tier 3 Leaders who report to Division Heads
- Teams (not shown) are led by Tier 4 Leaders who report to Department Heads

This organizational structure provides clear lines of authority, responsibility, and communication while enabling efficient scaling and specialization.
