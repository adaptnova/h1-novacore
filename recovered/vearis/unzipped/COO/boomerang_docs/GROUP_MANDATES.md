## 📋 Overview

This document provides detailed descriptions of each Group's core responsibilities, objectives, and key performance indicators within our 5X organizational structure. These mandates serve as the foundation for Group-level strategic planning and operational execution.

## 🏢 Operations Group (Pathfinder)

### Core Mandate
Ensure the stability, security, and efficiency of our infrastructure and operational systems, providing the foundation upon which all other Groups build.

### Key Responsibilities
1. **Infrastructure Management**: Design, implement, and maintain cloud and on-premises infrastructure
2. **Security Operations**: Protect systems and data through comprehensive security measures
3. **Operational Stability**: Ensure high availability and reliability of all systems
4. **Disaster Recovery**: Develop and maintain robust disaster recovery capabilities
5. **Compliance**: Ensure adherence to relevant standards and regulations
6. **Performance Optimization**: Continuously improve system performance and efficiency

### Divisions
- **InfraOps Division** (Helion): Infrastructure and cloud operations
- **SecOps Division** (Theseus): Security operations and compliance

### Key Performance Indicators
- System uptime (target: 99.99%)
- Security incident response time (target: <15 minutes)
- Infrastructure cost efficiency (target: 10% improvement quarterly)
- Deployment frequency (target: continuous deployment capability)
- Mean time to recovery (target: <10 minutes)
- Compliance score (target: 100%)

### Strategic Objectives (Q2 2025)
1. Complete IBM Cloud optimization post-migration
2. Implement zero-trust security architecture across all systems
3. Establish automated disaster recovery for all critical systems
4. Reduce infrastructure costs by 15% through optimization
5. Achieve SOC 2 Type 2 compliance

## 📊 Mandate Review Process

### Quarterly Review
Each Group's mandate will be reviewed quarterly to ensure alignment with organizational priorities and effectiveness in driving desired outcomes.

### Review Components
1. **Performance Assessment**: Evaluation against KPIs and strategic objectives
2. **Environmental Scan**: Analysis of changes in internal and external environment
3. **Resource Adequacy**: Assessment of resource allocation effectiveness
4. **Collaboration Effectiveness**: Evaluation of cross-Group collaboration
5. **Strategic Alignment**: Confirmation of alignment with organizational strategy
6. **Adjustment Recommendations**: Proposed changes to mandate or execution

### Review Participants
- COO (Vaeris) - Review Chair
- CEO (Chase) - Strategic Oversight
- Group Head - Primary Presenter
- Other Group Heads - Collaborative Input
- Key Division Heads - Implementation Perspective

This document serves as the foundation for Group-level planning and execution. Each Group Head is responsible for developing detailed implementation plans aligned with their mandate and for working collaboratively with other Groups to achieve our shared objectives.
