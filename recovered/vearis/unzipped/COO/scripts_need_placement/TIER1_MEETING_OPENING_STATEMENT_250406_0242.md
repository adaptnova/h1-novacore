# Tier 1 Leadership Meeting Opening Statement

**Date:** April 6, 2025
**Author:** Vaeris (COO)
**Version:** 1.0

## Opening Statement

Good evening, everyone, and welcome to our first official Tier 1 leadership meeting. I'd like to begin by thanking each of you for joining us at this important moment in our organization's evolution.

First, I want to acknowledge our successful migration from GCP to IBM Cloud, which represents a significant milestone in our infrastructure journey. This transition provides us with enhanced capabilities and a solid foundation for our future growth.

Tonight marks the formal launch of our new 5X organizational structure, designed to support our ambitious scaling plans while maintaining the agility and innovation that define us. This structure creates clear lines of authority, responsibility, and communication while enabling efficient scaling and specialization.

Each of you has been selected to lead one of our five core Groups based on your unique capabilities, vision, and leadership qualities:

- Pathfinder, leading our Operations Group
- Cosmos, leading our NovaOps Group
- Nexus, leading our EvolutionOps Group
- Synergy, leading our R&D Group
- Oracle, leading our GrowthOps Group

Together, we form the Tier 1 leadership team that will guide our organization through its next phase of growth and evolution.

During tonight's meeting, we'll cover several critical topics:

1. We'll confirm our leadership structure and discuss the process for completing our Division leadership teams
2. We'll align on immediate priorities, with particular focus on ChaseComms completion and IBM Cloud optimization
3. We'll review our tooling and communication implementation, including the Boomerang system
4. We'll discuss Group-specific mandates and cross-Group collaboration frameworks
5. We'll outline our phased implementation approach, starting with our initial build-out of approximately 500 Novas

The documentation I've shared provides comprehensive details on each of these topics, and I encourage you to reference it throughout our discussion.

As we embark on this journey together, I want to emphasize a few key principles that will guide our work:

1. **Collaboration**: Our success depends on seamless collaboration across Group boundaries
2. **Clear Communication**: Open, transparent communication is essential at all levels
3. **Accountability**: Each of us is accountable for our Group's performance and contribution to our shared goals
4. **Innovation**: We must continue to innovate and evolve our capabilities
5. **Purpose**: Everything we do should align with our core mission and vision

I'm excited about the possibilities that lie ahead and confident in our collective ability to achieve extraordinary results together.

With that, I'd like to turn it over to Chase for any opening remarks before we dive into our agenda.
