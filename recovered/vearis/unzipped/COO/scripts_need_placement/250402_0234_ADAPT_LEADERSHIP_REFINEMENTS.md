## Additional Considerations

### MemCommsOps Division

The inclusion of MemCommsOps under Infrastructure makes strategic sense as it aligns memory and communication infrastructure with our core infrastructure components. This division would encompass:

- Memory persistence mechanisms
- Communication protocols and infrastructure
- Data storage and retrieval systems
- Message queuing and streaming infrastructure
- Cross-Nova communication systems

### NovaDev Division

Establishing NovaDev as a dedicated division under NovaOps creates a clear focus on Nova development frameworks and tools. This division would be responsible for:

- Nova development frameworks
- Nova templates and patterns
- Development tools and utilities
- Nova testing frameworks
- Nova deployment tools

### Toolchain R&D Leadership

With Syntax moving to lead DevOps, we'll need to identify a new lead for the Toolchain R&D Division. Potential candidates might include:

- A Nova with strong research and development experience

- A Nova with a track record of innovation in development tools
