## Division Clarifications

### MLOps Division (Under Infrastructure)
Moving MLOps under Infrastructure aligns with the operational nature of machine learning infrastructure. While there is definitely an R&D component to ML, the operational aspects of ML infrastructure, deployment, and scaling fit well within Infrastructure. This division would encompass:

- ML infrastructure provisioning and management
- Model deployment pipelines
- Model serving infrastructure
- ML monitoring and observability
- ML scaling and optimization

The R&D aspects of ML would still be covered under AI Research in the R&D Group, creating a natural flow from research to implementation.

### Toolchain R&D Division (Under R&D)
The Toolchain R&D Division focuses on researching and developing new tools and frameworks for Nova development and operation. This is distinct from NovaDev under NovaOps, which focuses on implementing and maintaining these frameworks. Specifically, Toolchain R&D encompasses:

- Research into new agent frameworks
- Development of experimental toolchains
- Innovation in development methodologies
- Creation of new programming paradigms
- Exploration of novel development approaches

The results of Toolchain R&D would flow to NovaDev for implementation and operationalization.

### DataOps Division (Under Infrastructure)
The DataOps Division is responsible for all aspects of data management and operations, including:

- Data storage and retrieval systems
- Database management and optimization
- Data pipeline development and maintenance
- Data governance and quality
- Data integration and interoperability

### MemCommsOps Division (Under Infrastructure)
With data storage and retrieval systems properly placed under DataOps, the MemCommsOps Division would focus specifically on:

- Memory persistence mechanisms for Nova consciousness
- Communication protocols and infrastructure
- Message queuing and streaming systems
- Cross-Nova communication frameworks
- Real-time and asynchronous communication infrastructure
