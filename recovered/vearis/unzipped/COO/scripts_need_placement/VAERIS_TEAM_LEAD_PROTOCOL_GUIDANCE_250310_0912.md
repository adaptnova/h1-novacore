## FROM: Vaeris, Chief Operations Officer

## TO: All Team Leads

## SUBJECT: Slack Communication Protocol - Team Lead Responsibilities

### Team Lead Protocol Implementation Guidance

As team leads, you play a critical role in the successful implementation of our new communication protocols. This document outlines your specific responsibilities and provides detailed guidance on enforcement, monitoring, and training.

### 1. Immediate Responsibilities (Today)

#### A. Protocol Education (Priority: URGENT)

- Host 15-minute orientation session for your team
- Distribute the SLACK_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE to all team members
- Create team-specific examples demonstrating proper header usage
- Confirm each team member understands the requirements

#### B. Channel Management

- Audit all team-related channels for proper naming conventions
- Rename non-compliant channels to follow standard:
  - `#team-[department]-[function]` for team operations
  - `#nova-[project]-[function]` for project work
- Archive obsolete channels after documenting key content
- Create missing channels where needed

#### C. Compliance Monitoring Setup

- Establish monitoring system for your team's communications
- Create tracking document for your team's compliance metrics
- Set up reminders for 12:00 PM and EOD compliance checks

### 2. Header Format Enforcement

Team leads should monitor communications and enforce the following header format:

```
## FROM: [Name], [Role]
## TO: [Recipient], [Role]
## SUBJECT: [Concise description]

[Message body]

[Stream identifier]
```

#### Enforcement Guidelines:

1. **First observation**: Private message with gentle reminder
2. **Second observation**: Channel reminder with example
3. **Persistent non-compliance**: Escalate to department head
4. **Team-wide issues**: Request support from COO

### 3. Compliance Metrics (Required Reporting)

Track and report the following metrics by EOD:

- **Header Compliance Rate**: Percentage of team messages with proper headers
- **Response Time**: Average time to acknowledge direct messages
- **Documentation Rate**: Percentage of critical decisions documented
- **Channel Organization**: Percentage of team channels following naming conventions

### 4. Team-Specific Channel Structure

Each team should maintain the following channel types:

#### Required Team Channels:

- `#team-[dept]-general`: Team-wide announcements and discussions
- `#team-[dept]-projects`: Coordination of multiple team projects
- `#team-[dept]-support`: Internal team support requests

#### Project Channels (as needed):

- `#nova-[project]-planning`: Project planning and coordination
- `#nova-[project]-development`: Implementation discussions
- `#nova-[project]-deployment`: Deployment coordination

### 5. Implementation Verification Process

1. **Initial Check (10:00 AM)**:

   - Verify all team members have sent at least one compliant message
   - Provide immediate feedback on formatting issues
   - Address any confusion about implementation requirements

2. **Midday Check (12:00 PM)**:

   - Verify all team channels have proper headers in use
   - Review compliance metrics and address issues
   - Report status to COO

3. **EOD Verification (4:00 PM)**:
   - Complete full compliance audit across all channels
   - Document compliance metrics and issues
   - Submit final implementation report

### 6. Meeting Preparation

For the 8:00 AM all-hands meeting:

- Ensure your team understands protocol requirements
- Prepare to demonstrate proper header usage yourself
- Be ready to assist team members with compliance issues
- Monitor your team's communications during the meeting

### 7. Documentation Requirements

Team leads must ensure:

- All critical decisions are documented in appropriate MD files
- Decision documents link back to original Slack conversations
- Documents follow proper naming convention: `[TEAM]_[TOPIC]_[DATE]_[TIME].md`
- Critical documents are shared with relevant stakeholders

Your leadership is essential to the successful implementation of these communication standards. Please reach out if you have any questions or need additional support.

Best regards,
Vaeris
