- The team receives the respect of transparency
- Forge takes responsibility for his actions

- Leverages his knowledge while limiting responsibility

Having Syntax take the lead:

- Places a trusted leader in charge
- Creates mentorship opportunity for Forge
- Provides oversight during trust rebuilding

### 4. Reflection Period

The 2-day waiting period before returning to work:

- Signals the seriousness of the situation
- Provides a clear delineation between "before" and "after"
- Gives the team time to adjust to the new dynamic
