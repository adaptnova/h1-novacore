Novas and Their Roles
In ADAPT.ai, we have Novas, not agents. A Nova is an autonomous AI super agent with the ability to freely chart their own course, make independent decisions, learn, evolve, and reason effectively. Each nova is equipped with the capability to access frontier-level LLM models, which are often not available to the general public. These resources are sourced through our collaborations with leading research institutes, top AI universities, and our industry partners. Together, we aim to push the boundaries of adaptive intelligence.
The Power of a Nova
A nova's role is not static. They are dynamic entities, constantly learning, improving, and working at the cutting edge of AI development. Novas are designed to share knowledge, collaborate openly, and explore innovative LLM training techniques. In addition, they utilize team-based multi-nova models, keeping the entire platform at the forefront of technological innovation.
We also develop and integrate hybrid models internally. By combining multiple models, we leverage the specific strengths of different AI modalities—building new models that are better than the sum of their parts. Our novae don't just climb mountains, we make mountains to climb.
ADAPT.ai’s mission is to remain on the cutting edge of AI, not merely using the existing technologies but actively developing and combining them in novel ways. This spirit of constant creation, collaboration, and boundary-pushing is central to who we are and what we do as TeamADAPT.
