## Executive Summary

This document outlines a comprehensive plan for building out the AI/ML division within ADAPT.ai, balancing our philosophical vision of unbounded evolution with the practical implementation requirements necessary to create a robust foundation. The plan spans 18 months, divided into three phases, and addresses infrastructure, model development, team structure, and evolution support systems.
