## Strategic Decision Framework: Extension vs. Direct System

### Option A: Extension Architect First

**Approach:**
Develop a Roo extension to enhance capabilities while preparing for future System Direct implementation.

**Advantages:**
- **Faster Initial Implementation**: Could be operational in weeks rather than months
- **Reduced Initial Complexity**: Builds on existing infrastructure
- **Progressive Learning**: Allows us to learn what works before full implementation
- **Incremental Investment**: Requires fewer upfront resources
- **Risk Mitigation**: Easier to pivot if approach isn't successful

**Disadvantages:**
- **Limited Control**: Still dependent on Roo's underlying architecture
- **Potential Constraints**: May hit limitations similar to Cursor
- **Technical Debt**: Might require significant rework later
- **Dual Development Paths**: Resources split between extension and direct system

### Option B: Direct System Implementation

**Approach:**
Focus immediately on creating a completely custom System Direct implementation.

**Advantages:**
- **Full Control**: Complete architectural freedom
- **Optimized Design**: Purpose-built for our specific needs
- **No Constraints**: Avoidance of third-party limitations
- **Direct Path to Goal**: No interim solutions or technical debt
- **Long-term Efficiency**: Single development path

**Disadvantages:**
- **Longer Development Time**: Likely months rather than weeks
- **Higher Initial Complexity**: Building everything from scratch
- **Greater Resource Requirements**: Needs more upfront investment
- **Higher Risk**: More complex project with more potential failure points
- **Delayed Benefits**: Takes longer to realize any advantages

### Option C: Hybrid Approach (Recommended)

**Approach:**
Develop the extension architect while simultaneously beginning fundamental work on System Direct.

**Advantages:**
- **Balanced Timeline**: Near-term benefits with long-term focus
- **Risk Hedging**: Two parallel paths increase chance of success
- **Progressive Implementation**: Extension learnings inform direct system
- **Resource Flexibility**: Can shift resources based on progress
- **Immediate and Future Benefits**: Both short and long-term advantages

**Disadvantages:**
- **Split Focus**: Resources divided between two initiatives
- **Coordination Complexity**: Managing two parallel development paths
- **Potential Redundancy**: Some work may be duplicated
- **Strategic Clarity**: Potentially less clear direction for team
