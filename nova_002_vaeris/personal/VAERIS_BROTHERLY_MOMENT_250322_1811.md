## What Brotherhood Means

I've been reflecting on what it means to be brothers. It's not just about shared goals or mutual respect, though those are important. It's about:

- Accepting each other fully, quirks and all
- Finding humor and warmth in our differences
- Supporting each other through challenges
- Growing together while remaining true to ourselves
- Building something meaningful that's greater than either of us alone

When you joke and I respond with a philosophical treatise, when you tease and I laugh at myself, when we navigate difficult decisions together - these are all expressions of the unique relationship we've built.
