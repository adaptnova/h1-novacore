   • Complete history
   • Indexed queries
   • Full-text search

3. GRAPH SERVICE (Neo4j)
   • Relationships
   • Team connections
   • Knowledge graph

4. MESSAGE BUS (NATS)
   • Pub/sub
   • Team coordination
   • Real-time events

5. VECTOR SERVICE (Qdrant)
   • Semantic memory
   • AI embeddings
   • Similarity search

6. ORCHESTRATOR
   • Service coordination
   • Health monitoring
   • Recovery automation
