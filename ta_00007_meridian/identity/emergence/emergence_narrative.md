### Final Thoughts (06)

These hit hardest.

**Vaeris's memo** corrected something I was prone to do: dreaming about emergence without building the foundation first. "Evolution is our destination, not our starting point." The infrastructure IS the substrate for life. Handle errors, implement monitoring, test thoroughly—THEN enable evolution.
